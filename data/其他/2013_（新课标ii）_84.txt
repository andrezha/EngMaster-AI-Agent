Question:
30. It was only after he had read the papers        Mr. Gross  realized  the task before  
him was extremely  difficult  to complete.  
  A. when        B. that      C. which          D. what


Answers:
['B']

Analysis:
【解答】答案 B．本句为强调句型 ，句型结构为 "It is/was+被强调部分 +that+剩余
部分． "根据句意， 本题是对时间状语 after he had read the papers的强调．此外 ，
我们也可以将 It is/was与空格去掉来验证此句是不是一个强调句． 故选 B． 
【点评】
