Question:
25. I don’t believe  we’ve met before,          I must  say you do look familiar.  
A. therefore          B. although           C. since            D. unless


Answers:
['B']

Analysis:
【解答】 答案 B．A项"因此 "；B项"尽管，虽然 "；C项"既然，自从 "；D项"除
非"．因为主句的 "don ’t believe"与从句的 "do look familiar"意思相反 ，前后意思转
折，所以用表转折的连词， 故选 B项．
