Question:
33. Four  and    half hours  of discussion  took us up to midnight,  and    break  for 
cheese,  chocolate  and tea with sugar.  
A. a; a       B. the; the       C. 不填 ; the       D. a; 不填


Answers:
['A']

Analysis:

第二空 a break泛指 "一段休息时间 "． 
故选： A．
