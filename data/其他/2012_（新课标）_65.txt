Question:
26. The result  is not very important  to us, but if we do win, then so much         .  
A. the best          B. best              C. better           D. the better


Answers:
['D']

Analysis:
【解答】 答案 D．So much  the better常常和 if条件句连用，意为 "如果 …那就更
好了 "，其反义短语为 so much  the worse，意思是那就更糟糕了． 故选 D． 
【点评】
