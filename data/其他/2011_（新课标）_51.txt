Question:
27. The next thing  he saw was smoke  _______from  behind  the house.  
A. rose         B. rising          C. to rise      D. risen


Answers:
['B']

Analysis:
【解答】答案：B．本题考察非谓语动词的用法， 先分析句子的主干为 ：The next 
thing  was smoke．risng与所修饰的名词 smoke之间的关系是主动的关系， 故用
rising  from  behind  the house  做后置定语． 故选 B．
