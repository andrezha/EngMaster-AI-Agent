TITLE: Managing Distractions During an Internship
Interruptions are one of the biggest challenges when you are trying to complete your tasks. 36, there are several ways to handle the situation. Let us look at them now.
Tell the person you are sorry and explain that you have many things to finish, and then ask if you can talk at a different time.
When people try to interrupt you, have specific hours planned for meetings and let them know to come back then. 38. It can help to reduce future interruptions.
When you need to talk to someone, do not always do it at your own desk. 39, it is much easier to excuse yourself to get back to your work than if you try to get someone to leave your space after you have already explained how busy you are.
If you have a private office or a specific workspace, make good use of it. 40. If someone approaches you when you are busy, excuse yourself and let them know you are working so they understand that when you are focused, you should not be disturbed.
