TITLE: Rebuilding Trust in a Team Project
Trust is a behavior we learn from our past experiences. 36 Trust involves a risk. But a team cannot be successful when there is a lack of trust caused by an action where someone refuses to take responsibility for a mistake.
Unfortunately, we have all experienced a broken promise. Whether someone has shared our private ideas, lied about progress, or failed to do their part, there are different levels of losing trust. Sometimes people simply feel they cannot trust their teammates anymore. 37 It is understandable, but if you are willing to rebuild trust in a team, there are steps you can take.
◆ 38 Having confidence in your own abilities will help you make better choices because you can see what is best for the project and your own growth.
◆ 39 If you have been let down, you are a victim of the situation. But there is a difference between being a victim and living with a "victim mentality". At some point, we will all have our trust tested.
◆ You did not lose everything. Once trust is broken, what is left? Instead of looking at the situation as hopeless, look at what you still have and be thankful for the good parts of the project. 40 Instead, it is a healthy way to handle the experience to allow for professional growth and moving forward.
