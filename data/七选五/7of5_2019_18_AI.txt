TITLE: Professional Email Etiquette for Interns
In a professional internship, developing healthy ways of communicating with supervisors is very important. 36 While I have only listed a few examples, there are many other situations that can happen. Interns should be able to apply the logic of each to their own situation.
Do's
• 37 Questions about a task are generally welcomed. Before asking about a project, read the instructions and previous emails to be sure the answer is not already there.
• Participate in team chats and shared documents. 38 Be sure to stay on topic and do not share unrelated information. Make your point clearly and respect others' ideas.
Don'ts
• Do not share too much personal information. Supervisors are not your family members or best friends. If you need more time for a task, simply explain the situation. 39
• Do not openly express frustration about a supervisor or a task. 40 When an intern complains about a manager on social media, it says more about the intern's own professionalism. If there is a real concern, use the proper channels to offer your feedback calmly.
