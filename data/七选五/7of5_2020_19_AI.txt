TITLE: Adapting to a New Workplace Culture
We all want to feel comfortable and accepted in a new job. But at times it seems too difficult and out of reach. 36 Here are a few ways that will help you move in the right direction.
● 37 Do not focus on colleagues who make you feel like you are not doing enough. Why do you compare yourself to them? Are you hoping that you will feel better because you are working harder than they are? Know that your career is your own; you are a unique individual.
● Forgive yourself for mistakes. We are often embarrassed by our errors or failures. 38 You will make mistakes, especially when you are learning. Rather than worrying about how you could have done better, why not give yourself a kind response? "That did not go as planned, but I did my best."
● Recognize your strengths. Write them down. Train your mind to look at what you do well before looking at your weaknesses. List all your small wins. You finished a report, learned a new tool, or helped a teammate. 39
● Now that you have listed your strengths, list your challenges. Write down why you feel unsure or not good enough. Now, read these words to yourself. 40 Look back at your list of strengths and achievements. See how much you have already achieved?
