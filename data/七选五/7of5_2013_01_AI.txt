TITLE: Social Practice Presentation Training
·Find a mentor
71, so do not be afraid to ask for help. Since there are many organizations ready to offer you presentation training and workshops, here are some things to consider when choosing the training that is right for you.
·Focus on your strengths
Any training you do to become more effective at public speaking should always focus on the positive aspects of what you already do well. Nothing can damage your confidence more than being told only about your faults. 72, so good training should develop those skills instead of just listing what you should avoid.
·73
If you find a presentation course that seems to give you too many strict rules, look elsewhere! Your mind is already busy with the information you want to share. 74. As far as we are concerned, there are basically no fixed rules about speaking to an audience. Your listeners can be your friends.
·Be yourself, not a copy
Most importantly, good training should treat you as a unique individual with your own style. 75. Your training should help you show your true personality, not try to turn you into a different person.
