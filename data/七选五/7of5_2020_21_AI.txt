TITLE: Organizing a Welcome Party for New Interns
A welcome party is a special event held when someone joins a new team or office. The team leader or the department is usually the one who organizes the party. The event is a chance for colleagues to welcome the new person to the workplace. 36 It is also a good time to fill the new environment with a friendly atmosphere and helpful advice.
37 Some teams create a list of items the new intern might need for their desk. Common things might include notebooks, pens, or even a coffee mug. Even if there is no list, a good welcome gift is something to help the intern feel at home, like a small plant or a helpful book.
38 This is often appreciated since at a welcome party there is usually only light food. There are often no planned games at these events. The host will, however, probably give the new intern a tour of the office. Sometimes, because a party happens right after an intern starts, people might be asked to help them set up their computer. 39
Welcome parties are important because they help build a strong team from the start. 40 Now most offices use digital tools to stay connected, but the goal of making someone feel warm and welcome remains the same.
