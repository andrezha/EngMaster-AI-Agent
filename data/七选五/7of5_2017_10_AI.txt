TITLE: Transitioning to Group Study
If anyone had told me a year ago that I would be spending most of my evenings in group study sessions, I would have laughed. I used to think group study was for people who liked wasting time, loud talking, and slow progress. They had nothing in common with me. 36
The friends who first invited me to a group session thought it meant working in a very basic way. 37 We sat in a crowded library, shared one small desk, and had to walk a long way just to find a quiet spot. That first experience cost me a lot of time and left me with a headache and unfinished work.
I was, however, talked into trying another study group in a different setting. 38 Instead, we had a private room with comfortable chairs and a large screen. My organized friends had remembered to bring all the necessary materials.
39 We have studied together many times since then. Recently, we even started using an online platform to share notes and videos. We have a clear schedule and a very efficient way of working.
40 It must be true that sooner or later, everyone finds a way to work well with others. I recommend that you find your own style of group study.
