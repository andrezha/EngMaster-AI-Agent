TITLE: Managing Time for Campus Projects
From my experience, there are three main reasons why students struggle with projects: skill, budget, and time. 36 Money is a topic for another discussion. So today I want to share some advice on how to make the most of the time you spend on your tasks. Here are three tips for working on a tight schedule:
(1) Plan ahead. The moments when I feel a project is a burden are when I am already tired and nothing is prepared. So think about the coming week. When will you have time to work? Do you have the right notes ready? 37
(2) Make your time count. When you do find time to work on a task, make the most of it to save time later. Are you writing one report? 38 It takes about the same amount of effort to organize more information at once. So save yourself the trouble for a future assignment.
(3) 39 This might surprise you, but one of the best ways to make your work efficient is to try new methods. It gives you the chance to find new ideas that work well with your style and schedule. The more you learn and the more you try, the more power you have over your work and your time.
Hopefully, that gives you a good start. 40 And do not let a busy schedule stop you from making great changes in the way you work and study!
