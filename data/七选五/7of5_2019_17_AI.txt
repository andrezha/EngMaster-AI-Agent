TITLE: Motivation for Internship Tasks
Imagine an intern standing before a difficult task and asking, "Should I try this?" This is what motivation, or the lack of it, can do. Motivation and goal setting are two parts of the same process. 36 Like the intern facing the task, you will remain stuck.
37 More than that, how should you stay motivated to reach your goal? First, you need to evaluate yourself—your skills, your strengths, and your weaknesses. Only then should you set your targets.
You also need to judge the strength of your motivation. This is very important because it is directly related to your effort. There are times when you do not feel like working. 38 So, slow down and think about what you really want to achieve. Clear thinking can help you move forward.
Another way of setting realistic goals is to analyze your short-term and long-term plans. Remember that goals are flexible. 39 They also need to be something you can measure. Keep these points in mind while planning.
Your personal situation is also important. For example, you may want to lead a project but cannot because you lack specific experience. 40 You should look at your goals again and motivate yourself to set a new one.
