TITLE: The Culture of Campus Clubs
Before there were formal classes, there was the tradition of gathering in groups. Joining a club expresses shared interests, creativity, teamwork, and everything in between.
36 Campus clubs are everywhere. We see them from the sports fields to the science labs, from arts to technology. We meet at weekly sessions, competitions, and social events just to share our passions.
"I love being part of the debate club," says Mark Evans, a student leader. "I cannot imagine my college life without it." Evans organizes activities for students of all backgrounds. "Leading a club is wonderful. 37 It is great to see them grow. For many, it is a way of meeting new people and building a social life."
38 "I can tell you about two new members," says Evans. "They are learning how to present their ideas. They arrive at the meeting feeling nervous and they leave with confidence. 39"
So, do we join clubs to make ourselves feel more connected and skilled? Sarah Jenkins says, "Being in a club is like being part of a team. Even after years of participating, I want to get better and better. 40 I find it hard to stop! It reminds me that I am part of a community."
