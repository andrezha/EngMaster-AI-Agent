TITLE: Digital Privacy and Information Security
Secret codes keep our digital messages private. Banks, tech companies, and government offices use these codes to protect data, especially when information is shared online.
People have used hidden messages for thousands of years. 36 Finding ways to read secret messages is always a challenge for those who create them. The study of making and reading these codes is known as cryptography.
There are three main types of this science. 37 For example, the first letters of "Send every new data" spell out the hidden word "Send." 38 You might replace each letter with a specific symbol or number. For instance, if we give each letter a number from 1 to 26, the word "Act" would become "1 3 20."
A real code uses symbols to represent whole words or phrases. To understand such a message, you must have a special code book. 39 For example, "blue" might mean "start" and "red" might mean "stop." The message "Blue red" would actually mean "Start stop." 40 However, it is also difficult to keep a code book safe for a long time. Therefore, codes must be updated often.
