TITLE: Workplace Wellness: Four Steps for Managing Internship Stress
Everyone feels pressure from time to time, especially when starting a new internship. 71 Some ways of dealing with stress—like complaining loudly or avoiding tasks—don’t solve much. But other ways, like talking to a mentor you trust, can lead you to solving your problem or at least feeling more relaxed.
Try taking these four steps the next time you are stressed at work:
(1) Get support. When you need help, reach out to the people who guide you. Talk to a trusted professional, such as a supervisor or a senior colleague. 72 They might have faced similar challenges, such as dealing with a difficult project or a tight deadline.
(2) Don’t be too hard on yourself. Sometimes when interns are stressed and upset, they blame themselves for every small mistake. That is not a good idea. Remember that there are always people around to help you. 73
(3) Try to solve the problem. After you are calm and you have support from colleagues and friends, it is time to get down to business. 74 Even if you cannot solve the whole issue at once, you can handle a small part of it.
(4) Be positive. Most workplace stress is temporary. Remember that pressure does go away, especially when you find the cause and start working on a solution. These steps are not magic, but they do work. And if you can stay positive as you work your way through a tough time, you will help yourself feel better even faster. 75
