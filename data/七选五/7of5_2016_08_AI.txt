TITLE: Designing a Personal Study Space
Have you ever spent time in a study area that felt just right for you, where the environment seemed to help you focus perfectly? 36. But this does not happen by chance. It starts with looking at your own habits and understanding how you interact with your surroundings and how you prefer to work.
