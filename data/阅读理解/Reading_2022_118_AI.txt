TITLE: Drones: The Future of Bridge Safety
Can a few small drones help keep our bridges safe and save cities millions of dollars? This is likely the future of bridge inspection. Today’s technology can monitor thousands of bridges 24/7 to make sure they are safe for cars and trucks.
Drones are already used to check power lines. They can do the same for bridges, looking for cracks or other problems in places that are hard for humans to reach. The more often bridges are checked, the safer they become. This would also make the work much faster and cheaper.
Maintenance costs for bridges are very high. For example, some cities spend millions every year to send workers high up on cables to check for rust. This can be very dangerous work. Drones can do this work instead, keeping workers safe on the ground.
In the future, drones will be even smarter. They will have sensors to find problems before they become dangerous. Some engineers are even designing drones that can "live" on the bridge and fly out automatically every day to check for any changes. This early warning system will help prevent accidents and keep traffic moving smoothly.
