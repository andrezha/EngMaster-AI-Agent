TITLE: Explore Global Heritage with Experts
Ancient Greece (12 days)
Travel across the historic lands of Greece with Dr. Helena R. Vance. We will explore some of the most famous ruins in the Mediterranean. Our journey also provides a chance to visit the birthplaces of great philosophers. Blue seas, white-stone villages and local food make this a memorable trip.
Northern Italy (14 days)
Italy offers beautiful lakes and the smell of fresh grapes, but it is also a center of historic treasures including sites left by the Romans and Renaissance artists. As we move from Milan with Prof. James Miller to historic Venice and into Florence, we study ancient buildings and art.
Japan’s Cultural Heart (20 days)
Discover the Japan of “old times,” its wooden castles, gardens and forest paths with Prof. Kenji Sato. Highlights include Japan’s most famous shrines at Kyoto and Nara’s peaceful temples and traditional crafts. We will walk through quiet mountain villages and finish our trip at a modern gallery in Tokyo.
Egypt (16 days)
Join Prof. David Grant on our detailed Egyptian tour. Tour highlights include the Great Pyramid of Giza, the valley of the Kings at Luxor, and the remote temples near Aswan, famous for their giant statues. Our journey takes us to traditional markets and the banks of the Nile.
