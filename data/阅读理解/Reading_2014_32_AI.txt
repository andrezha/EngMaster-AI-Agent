TITLE: The Youth Innovation Challenge
Join the Youth Innovation Challenge!
The Regional Design Council (RDC) is proud to announce the seventh annual Innovation Challenge. This event encourages, and even dares, students aged 7 to 15 to create a digital project or a short essay that demonstrates their creative thinking and how it helps them solve everyday problems.
Students are invited to design a simple app, write a proposal, take a series of photos, or create a video that shows what they are passionate about. To participate, all digital projects or written entries should be uploaded to the official portal or sent to the Regional Design Council, Innovation Lab, 450 Tech Way, Seattle 98101, by Friday, March 15th.
Participants who are selected as winners will be recognized at a formal awards ceremony during the Design Festival on Saturday, May 18th. A guest speaker from a leading tech firm will also give out prizes. The best projects will be featured in a digital magazine. Students' work will be put on display, and various awards will be presented. The families of participants are invited to the celebration, where a special lunch will be provided.
Between April 10th and April 15th, winners will receive detailed information about the final ceremony and the celebration. Further details and rules can be found at: http://youthinnovationchallenge.org.
