TITLE: Rethinking Genius in a Modern World
Who is a genius? For a long time, we thought of geniuses only as famous men from the past, like Einstein. But the nature of genius is actually much broader.
In the past, the standards for being a “genius” were often set by a small group of people. Because of this, many women and people from different backgrounds were ignored, even when they made great discoveries. A recent study showed that even young children are affected by these old ideas. By age seven, some girls begin to avoid activities that are described as being for “very smart” people.
The good news is that in today’s connected world, we can see genius everywhere. Social factors like race or gender do not decide who is smart. Future geniuses will come from anyone with hard work, creativity, and a bit of luck.
