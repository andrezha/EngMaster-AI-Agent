TITLE: Art of the Renaissance Exhibition
The Exhibition
This exhibition features fifty major works by the famous Italian artist, Leonardo da Vinci. Coming to New York from galleries worldwide, it is the first time these pieces have been shown together in thirty years.
Lectures
The Metropolitan Museum offers free lectures for the public. They take place in the Main Hall. No ticket is required for the lectures.
Opening Hours
Tuesday-Saturday 09.00-18.00 Sunday 11.00-18.00
Last entry is at 17.30. No re-entry allowed.
Closed: Thanksgiving, Christmas Day, and New Year’s Day.
Tickets
Standard: $15. Children under 10 with an adult enter for free.
Student Groups: A special price of $5 per person is available for full-time students in groups of ten or more with a teacher.
