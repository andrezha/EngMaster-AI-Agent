TITLE: Elena’s Dream Trip to the Amazon
As Elena reached the Amazon rainforest, she was greeted by the sound of colorful birds singing in the trees. These beautiful creatures made her feel welcome and started a journey she would never forget.
Since she was a girl, Elena, now 68, has loved exploring new places. During her years as a teacher, she traveled around her own country but always wanted to go further. When she retired and her children were grown, she decided it was time to take a big step.
After taking a short course in photography, Elena began to travel the world. Eventually, she found a way to get a cheap seat on a small boat going deep into the Amazon. “I just knew I had to go,” she says. “I didn't know exactly what to expect, but I wasn't afraid. I wanted to see it for myself, and I wanted to go alone.”
In April 2012, Elena joined a group of travelers she had never met to start her adventure. “From seeing the rare plants to watching the sunsets over the river, it was all incredible. The Amazon is like nowhere else on Earth,” Elena says. “I remember seeing a jaguar for the first time; it moved so quietly through the forest. It was a moment of pure magic.”
She realized that this is a very special place that people must protect. This was the most important thing Elena learned on her trip.
