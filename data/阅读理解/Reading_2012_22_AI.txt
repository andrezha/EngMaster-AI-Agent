TITLE: A Glimpse into the High-Tech Simulation
About fifteen of us were lucky enough to receive invitations to a modern tech lab to join a simulation test. Although our participation would last only for a short period, we could see many interesting things.
We all stood at one end of the experimental room while staff prepared the setting, placing artificial trees along a curved path. Very soon, bright studio lights were turned on and a large professional camera was moved into position. The supervisor gave some instructions to the technician and then went to talk to the two lead researchers nearby. Since it was quite warm in the lab, it surprised us to see one researcher put on a thick winter coat and start walking along the path. A powerful machine began blowing small white foam bits down on him, and soon the trees were covered in "frost". Two more fans were started, and a "strong chilly wind" blew through the area. The scene looked so realistic that it actually made us feel cold.
The next part of the test was a total contrast. The way it was recorded was quite special. Videos taken on a tropical beach were displayed on a large high-definition screen. Two staff members stood in front of the screen so that they appeared to be standing at the water’s edge on a sunny coast. By a simple visual trick like this, palm trees, golden sand, and bright blue skies had been brought into the closed room!
Since it was our turn next, we were left wondering what kind of environment would be set up for us. For a full three minutes, we would be experiencing the thrill of being the "main characters" in this experiment!
