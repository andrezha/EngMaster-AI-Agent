TITLE: A Laptop and a Lesson in Kindness
Moving to London on his own from Malaysia, my husband, Adam, stayed in a temporary apartment while looking for a permanent home for our family.
During his first week there, he went out one afternoon to visit a local library. He returned in the evening only to realize that his backpack was missing. He was incredibly worried because the backpack contained his laptop, which held all his work files, digital keys, and contact information. He reported the loss to the local authorities and then sat in his room, feeling stressed and alone in a new city, thinking about how difficult it would be to replace all that data while trying to start a new job.
Late that night, his phone rang. It was a stranger. The man was struggling to say my husband’s name and asked several questions to confirm his identity. Then he explained that his teenage son had found a backpack in a recycling bin that had been left on the sidewalk.
My husband drove to their house and found a friendly family waiting with his laptop and all his belongings. Their son had noticed the bag in the bin and thought it looked too good to be thrown away. Her parents had looked through the bag, even though it was filled with professional documents they didn't quite understand. Finally, they found a printed resume in the side pocket where my husband had written his local mobile number.
That family not only returned the valuable electronics to us that night but also restored our belief in the goodness of strangers. We still think of their honesty and often wish them well.
