TITLE: Robotics Day: More Than Just a Science Event
If you are a tech enthusiast — or would like to become one — take advantage of Robotics Day to see what is happening in the field. It is called Robotics Day but in reality, it is more like Robotics Month. The main day is on November 15, but since it has become popular, events now happen throughout the whole month across the country.
Attending a robotics event is a great chance to see, and often test, a wide range of machines. For people who are used to the basic models like vacuum robots found in stores, it can be a real surprise to see the variety of industrial robots still in use, such as the "Mark-1" which was first built in the 1970s. Although it doesn't do anything complex today, it is still worth seeing, as is the strange "Spider-Bot" which is more of an unusual project than a practical tool.
There are also robots developed to handle specific local tasks. One of the most impressive models for precision work is the "Nano-Hand," but you will need a perfectly clean, high-tech lab to operate it, so it is a "pipe dream" for most hobbyists who find it interesting.
At the events, you can meet expert engineers and discuss which projects will best suit your interests, and because these are family events, children are well provided for with robot-themed games and competitions.
Robotics Days are held at all kinds of locations with an interest in technology, including university labs and private tech companies. If you want a real hands-on experience, try visiting the National Tech Center in Silicon Valley.
