Innovations in Design
The Great Design Studio presents a talk by David Lee, in which a digital artist invites young tech fans and their teachers to his studio. Anna Smith hosts. Previews available. Opens March 5. (Global Tech Center, 200 Main St. 555-0123.)
The Visionary
Susan Hall stars in this presentation by Mark Evans, about the life of a famous tech pioneer and her meetings with world leaders over fifty years. Directed by James Wood. Also featuring Robert Brown. Starts March 10. (Innovation Hall, 150 West St. 555-4567.)
The Startup
Sarah Miller wrote this story about the early days of a tech company, where the growth of an idea is shown as a journey of teamwork. Directed by Paul King. Opens March 15. (Community Hub, 300 East St. 555-7890.)
Green Tech Today
John White and Linda Green star in this documentary about an engineer who tries to build an eco-friendly car during a journey across the country. Directed by Mary Scott. Previews begin March 5. (Modern Theater, 100 South St. 555-2345.)
