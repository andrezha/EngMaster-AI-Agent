TITLE: DIY Air Moisture Collector
A build-it-yourself air moisture collector is one of the smartest ways to get clean water in dry areas where liquid is hard to find. Created by engineers for outdoor survival, it is an excellent water gatherer. However, you must bring the right materials with you, as it is nearly impossible to find natural replacements in the wild. The only parts needed are a large sheet of clear plastic, some thin rubber tubing, and a small container to hold the water. These items can be folded into a tiny bag and carried easily.
To build a working collector, use a tool to dig a hole about four feet wide and three feet deep. Try to find a spot with slightly damp soil to help the water gatherer produce more. Place your container in the deepest part of the center. Then put the tube in place so that one end stays inside the container and the other end goes up and out of the hole.
Next, cover the hole with the plastic sheet. Fix the edges of the plastic with heavy soil and put a small stone in the center of the sheet to weigh it down. The plastic should now look like a deep bowl. The lowest point of the plastic must be directly over the container, and no more than a few inches above it.
The collector works by trapping heat under the plastic. Moisture in the ground turns into vapor and collects on the underside of the sheet until small drops form. These drops then slide down the plastic and fall into the container. When the container is full, you can drink the water through the tube without having to move the plastic every time you are thirsty.
