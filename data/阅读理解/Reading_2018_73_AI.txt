TITLE: Success on a Budget
Channel 5’s Linda Green is usually seen interviewing experts on business trends, but she is taking on a fresh role — showing young professionals how to build a successful career without spending a fortune on expensive courses.
In Smart Career: Great Future, she visits a different workplace each week. With the help of career coach David Miller, she offers top tips on how to improve professional skills while keeping costs under £10 a week. The presenter says she has been able to use many of these tips herself while mentoring her junior staff.
“We all want high-end professional certificates, so I used to pay for them online from top global institutes,” she explains. “I paid £500 for one short course, but David shows how to gain the same knowledge for £5 through local workshops and open resources. Everyone wants to move up, but sometimes we aren’t aware how cheaply we can improve our skills ourselves.”
The six-part series follows the success of Channel 5’s Smart Life: Great Health, which gave viewers advice on how to get the best value from the wide range of fitness products on the market.
With training being a major career expense, Linda and David spend time with a different individual each week. In tonight’s episode, they help a young worker in need of career direction on a tight budget. The team changes the worker’s outlook with less expensive but effective learning methods.
