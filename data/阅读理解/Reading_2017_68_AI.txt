TITLE: City Tour Experiences
Guided City Tours
Local Garden & Tea Tour
Dates: March 1st to May 30th
This peaceful tour travels through the historic district to the Riverside Park where we will visit the famous Green Tea House. Here you can taste four types of the finest local tea. (Included in the price)
Starting point: Central Square. Times available upon request.
Time: 2 hours
Price: $85
Vintage 1960s Tour
Dates: July 1st to July 20th
This tour takes you back to one of the city's most exciting times, the 1960s! Learn about local history as we drive you around the old town for a free sample of traditional snacks.
Starting point: Central Square. 4:00 pm and 6:00 pm
Time: 2 hours
Price: $85
Mystery Night Tour
Dates: October 15th to October 31st
Join us for a trip through the historic North District. Professional outdoor gear is provided to keep you warm as our guides take you to the most mysterious spots in the city.
Starting point: Central Square. 7:00 pm and 9:00 pm
Time: 1 hour and 30 minutes
Price: Please contact us for details
Winter Festival Tour
Dates: December 5th to December 25th
This lovely tour visits the city's brightest holiday light displays. Professional gear is provided to ensure you stay warm while enjoying the holiday atmosphere.
Starting point: Central Square. 6:30 pm and 8:30 pm
Time: 1 hour and 30 minutes
Note: You must book your place in advance.
