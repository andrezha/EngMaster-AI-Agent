TITLE: Winter Break Workshops
Students should review this list with their parents and choose two workshops they wish to attend. Registration forms are available at the office. Parents must sign to confirm the choices.
Wilderness Survival (WS)
Leave your house for a week to learn how to survive in the wild. You will learn new skills such as building a shelter and starting a fire. Activities include hiking and camping in the snowy hills. Work in a team and enjoy the winter environment.
Instructor: Mr. Brown | Cost: £130
European History (EH)
On Monday we travel to Berlin. After staying overnight, we spend Day 2 visiting historic sites from the Cold War. On Day 3 we travel to Prague. Thursday takes us to a famous castle for a tour and a traditional dinner. Our final day, Friday, is spent exploring the city center of Prague.
Instructor: Ms. Taylor | Cost: £410
Digital Arts (DA)
Four days of learning how to create digital art and animations. Using modern software to design your own characters and short films. Learn technical skills and finish the week with your own digital portfolio.
Instructor: Mrs. Green | Cost: £40
Movie Magic (MM)
Visit a famous film studio, stop to buy snacks for a picnic, and stay overnight in a hostel in the countryside. Enjoy a guided tour of famous filming locations from popular movies. Go on a short boat trip before returning home the next day.
Instructor: Miss Clark | Cost: £145
