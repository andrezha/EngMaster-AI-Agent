TITLE: The Natural Power of Greenery
Science may not fully explain the influence of houseplants, but many studies have shown that having green plants can help reduce mental fatigue and improve the speed of recovering from stress, decrease feelings of loneliness, and promote a positive mood. Any owner will tell you how much peace a small garden brings. For some, a plant provides more comfort than a busy friend.
A recent study measured stress levels and focus in people—half of them had plants on their desks—while they completed 10 minutes of difficult coding tasks. Subjects completed the tasks alone, with a colleague, or with a potted plant nearby. People with plants performed the best. Those working with greenery nearby had a smaller change in heart rate and returned most quickly to a calm state. With plants in the room, people also made fewer errors than when working in front of others. It seems people feel more relaxed around nature, as plants do not create pressure or judge performance.
Another report suggests that keeping a garden not only improves your mood but may also change your physical activity. Researchers spent a year studying 40 office workers and their small desk gardens; a separate group of 60 people without plants was also tracked. On average, those with plants spent more time moving around to care for their greenery. While they did not lose significantly more weight than those without plants, researchers noted they got more overall movement—mostly watering and pruning—and found it improved their work-life balance.
