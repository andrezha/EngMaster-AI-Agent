TITLE: Top Recommended Apps
Alex Chen is a tech reviewer and co-creator of the Digital Life series. Here he picks his top apps for productivity and learning.
MindFlow
By Sarah Jenkins
I once read a report on how mental focus affects productivity, and this app gave me a new way to practice it. MindFlow’s tools for managing stress and focus are both helpful and engaging. They really help users stay calm while working on tough projects.
Night Shift
By Kenji Sato
This app is about two modes—Focus, for deep work when you can’t be interrupted, and Relax, for resting. By using both, users start changing their daily habits and discover a world of "focus techniques" that were previously hidden.
Gone Digital
By Emma Vance
I didn't want to like this app when everyone else was using it, but the interface is brilliant. There is a sense of order from the start as you organize your tasks. It’s a real time-saver, and the satisfaction when you see your progress is truly rewarding.
The Bridge
By Marcus King
This is an excellent learning tool from one of the best educators around. After a major update, the app now covers 99% of common coding languages. A competition unfolds between learners as they solve challenges. The "Challenge Mode" is one of the most intense features ever.
