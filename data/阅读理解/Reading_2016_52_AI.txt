TITLE: Campus Weekend Guide
Modern Beats
8:00pm–1:30am Free at the North Hall
Do you enjoy local music? We are hosting an energetic night of live indie and electronic music from the city’s top student bands. Are you dreaming of starting a band or finding a manager? If so, arrive early for the 8:00pm workshop by Marcus Reed, a veteran music agent. He will discuss how to build a professional image for your music.
The Laugh Factory
9:00pm–11:00pm Comedy at the Student Center
Watch the famous Leo Brooks perform. He is widely considered the most creative comedian on campus. This high-energy show is designed for everyone, from freshmen to faculty members. Leo really knows how to keep an audience laughing! The cafe area opens at 7:30pm for light snacks and coffee.
Creative Writing Workshop
4:30pm–7:00pm Thursdays at the Library Annex
This is a perfect opportunity for anyone interested in scriptwriting or storytelling. The session covers various writing styles and offers practical exercises in building interesting characters. Sarah, a published author with 12 years of teaching experience, leads the class. These sessions are lively and helpful, giving you the tools to write with confidence.
Jazz Night with Mia
8:30pm–11:30pm The Blue Note Cafe
Great food and smooth jazz; this is an ideal way to spend your evening. Mia Vance will perform tracks from her latest hit album, accompanied by David on the saxophone. The cafe serves a variety of French dishes, including fresh pastries and gourmet salads. Reservations are recommended. The bar is open from noon, serving juices, tea, and soda.
