TITLE: Creative Places for Curious Kids
Are you looking for some new and exciting places to take your kids? Try some of these activities:
•Visit Art Workshops. They offer a variety of projects to spark your kids’ imagination. Many offer classes for making clay figures, temporary exhibits, book signings by famous illustrators, and even small concerts.
•Head to a Space Exploration Center. This is where kids can discover the universe from rocket models to planet maps and videos of astronauts. Also, ask what kind of educational programs are prepared for kids and any stargazing events that are coming up.
•Go to a DIY Studio. Look for one in your area offering crafts for young visitors. "Make-and-take" sessions are led by local makers where kids can discover the secret of building things. Model crafting and digital drawing are just a couple of the special offerings you might find.
•Try Practical Science. Visit one of the many DIY science labs around the country. These labs are great fun for kids and adults alike. They will keep your child mentally and physically active the whole day through while building circuits, experimenting with water, and constructing bridges. When everyone is tired, enjoy a science documentary, commonly found in these labs.
