TITLE: Smart Bike-Sharing Guide
Usage Rules
Each user needs a digital pass to unlock and lock a bike. Up to two children under age seven may ride for free on child-seat bikes with a paying adult.
Digital pass machines are located at every major hub. Please bring small notes or a credit card because there are no change machines at the hubs, and the pass machines only provide a maximum of $5 in change.
Get a "Day Rider" pass for unlimited 30-minute trips in one day. Buy it at any hub station. Use it after 8:30 a.m. on weekdays and any time on weekends and holidays.
Service Hours
Opens: 6 a.m. Mon. --- Fri. 8 a.m. Sat. --- Sun.
Closes: 10 p.m. Sun. --- Thur. Midnight Fri. --- Sat. nights
Hub status may vary. To ensure you can return a bike, please check the real-time hub map on the mobile app.
Standard Fees
When paying per ride, the fee is $1.50. When using a "SmartLink" card, the fee is $1.25.
Discounts for Students and Seniors
Senior citizens over 65 and students with valid IDs can ride for half the standard price. On bikes and at hubs, use a registered Senior/Student SmartLink card.
For more information about getting these cards and passes, please visit CityBikeService.com or call 555-100-2000. Senior and student users can also get a free printed guide on how to use the system by calling 555-100-3000.
Travel Tips
• Avoid riding during weekday rush hours --- specifically between 7:30 and 8:30 a.m. and between 5 and 6:30 p.m.
• If you leave a personal item on a bike or at a hub, please call the Support Center at 555-100-4000.
