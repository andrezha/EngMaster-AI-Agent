TITLE: Unique Eco-Camps Around the World
Sky Canopy Camp
The Sky Canopy Camp lets you live in a luxury treehouse. Six towers make up this camp that offers over 50 units. If you really want the full experience, book the Eagle Nest which is large enough for a big group. You will be 40 feet up in the air and can travel between the units through their hanging bridges.
Prices: starting at $250 a night for each person for a standard unit and up to $2000 for the Eagle Nest.
For more info, visit: http://www.skycanopy.com
The Arctic Pod
Every winter in Northern Norway, a special kind of camp called the Arctic Pod is built. Each year, famous artists are invited to design and create ice sculptures, many of which are placed in the pods. You can choose between heated or unheated pods, but staying at least one night in an unheated one is advised for a real adventure.
Prices: starting at $350 a night for each person for either a heated or unheated pod.
For more info, visit: http://www.arcticpod.com
Art-Pod Village
Art-Pod Village is a very special camp designed by a group of local artists. Every room provides you with the chance to live inside a piece of art. Every piece of furniture in the twenty rooms of the camp has been handmade and each room is unique. You can choose a room based on your own style.
Prices: starting at just $100 a night, and an additional person for only 20 extra dollars.
For more info, visit: http://www.artpodvillage.com
For information about other cool camps in the world, visit: http://www.naturestay.com
