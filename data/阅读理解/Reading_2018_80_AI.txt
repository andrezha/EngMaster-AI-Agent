TITLE: Visit Oak Valley Estate
Visitor Information
How to Get There
By Car: Follow the signs on Highway B12. Driving times: West Town—20 minutes, Easton—30 minutes, Riverside—50 minutes, Manchester—90 minutes.
By Train: The nearest station is Oak Valley North with regular services to major city centers.
Opening Times
Monday—Friday (closed on weekends) 10:00 am—5:00 pm, April 1st to October 31st.
Admission Prices
House & Park | Park Only
Adults: £13.00 | £7.50
Groups: £10.00 | £5.00
Special Events
Farmers’ Fair | May 10th
Come and taste fresh local produce and homemade drinks. Meet the farmers and get new cooking ideas.
Estate Flower Show | June 15th
This event celebrates its 20th year with a display of the finest flowers, making it a very popular event for garden lovers.
Charity Garden Day | September 5th
Oak Valley opens its private gardens to raise money for local schools. For a small gift, you can take a tour with a professional guide.
Christmas Market | November 20th
A great event for everyone. Explore the many stalls selling crafts while enjoying live music and street performances.
