TITLE: Testing Tech for a Living
When you are testing a new app on the rainy streets of Seattle, the noise of the city seems far away. Wait! This is not just fun—you have a report to finish!
Here lies the challenge for tech reviewer and software critic Leo Vance. “I always loved gadgets and always liked to find bugs, but it never occurred to me that I could make a living doing both,” Vance said. Now you can find his reviews everywhere—on major tech blogs, in magazines, or in his latest book, The Simple Guide to Modern Apps.
His career began about six years ago. After finishing a degree in Computer Science in Canada, he took a technical test for a major software firm, passed it, and got the job. After working there, Vance worked for a while at a startup in London, then a global firm in New York, where he fell so in love with the tech scene in the Northwest that he moved to Seattle.
Now as a professional reviewer, he spends a third of his year traveling to tech shows. The rest of the time is spent testing software and writing reviews of new devices at his home in Seattle.
As exciting as the job sounds, the hard part is testing—checking every single function. Sure, it is great to write about a new feature, but you had better get the technical details right or you could really frustrate a user who relies on your advice.
