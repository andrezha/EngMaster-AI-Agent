TITLE: Monthly Seminars at the Tech Museum
Our monthly seminars begin at 18:30 on the first Tuesday of every month except July. Admission is included in the standard entry fee and no pre-registration is required. They typically finish by 20:00.
October 5th
The Silicon Pioneers, by David Grant. Robert Noyce is considered one of the most influential early computer chip designers. He was also instrumental in mentoring others in the field of electronics and circuit design. David Grant will discuss how Noyce made such a significant impact on the training of early tech engineers.
November 2nd
Energy for the City, by Helen Clark. Long before the widespread use of solar power, there was a constant need for efficient coal and gas storage for heating. Helen will explain the history of urban energy systems and the development of early power plants, and how the city's power grid expanded over time.
January 4th
The Future of the High-Speed Rail, by Mark Evans. The West Coast Line is currently being upgraded for faster travel. The East Coast expansion project will take a bit more time. We will provide a report on the current progress and future plans.
February 1st
Riverside Architecture, by Sarah Jenkins. The city has many unique buildings along the river. Sarah has conducted a detailed study of all of them. She will share the most interesting and historic designs with us.
