TITLE: Innovation Center Visitor Guide
◆Visit the Innovation Center Shop
Be sure to visit the Innovation Center Shop during your tour to find unique tech kits or souvenirs to remember your trip. The shop is found on the second floor of the North Wing, right next to the VR Lab.
◆Dining Options
Our exhibits will inspire your mind, but we also care for your physical energy. Our cafe provides a full menu of healthy meals and snacks, as well as monthly specials. The cafe is on the second floor of the South Wing and remains open until 30 minutes before the Center closes.
◆Equipment Rental
Storage boxes are available for your personal items during your visit. These are located in the Main Hall near the Welcome Desk and in the North Wing. Drones for testing and tablets for guided tours can be rented at the Welcome Desk. A valid I.D. is required.
◆Support the Innovation Center
Since 1995, the Innovation Center has been fostering a love for creative design and modern technology. Today, we serve over 800,000 visitors a year and provide hands-on tech workshops to schools and community centers throughout the city. This is a significant achievement that we cannot maintain without the generous help of individuals and local businesses. Visit our website to see how you can support our mission.
