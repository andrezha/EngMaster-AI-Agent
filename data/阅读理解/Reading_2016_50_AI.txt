TITLE: The Rare Book Messenger
I am Robert Miller, a volunteer data courier. Since January 2018, I’ve completed 75 assignments—of those, 40 involved traveling across borders. I have exactly 48 hours to transport sensitive physical hard drives in my secure case because I use specific cooling packs that only last that long. In total, from the moment the data is encrypted to the time it must be delivered to the research facility, we have at most 72 hours. Thus, I am always aware of the clock.
I had one assignment last summer where I was delayed by a massive storm in Europe. I collected the drives in Berlin and was supposed to fly to Paris then back to London. But when I reached the desk in Berlin, the staff member said: “I’m very sorry, there’s a problem—all flights to Paris have been canceled.” So I took my case, placed it on the counter, and said: “In this case is critical research data that is needed immediately for a medical project—please, you have to help me get to the UK.” She immediately focused on my problem. She found a seat on a small private cargo flight, changed my route through Brussels, and got me back to London even faster than my original plan.
In this messenger role, you are constantly aware that inside that case, you are carrying something that could potentially change the future of medicine.

[ANALYSIS]
29. B. Contextual meaning. Robert describes his "assignments" as "trips" to "transport" and "deliver" items, making him a delivery person.
30. D. Detail. He states he has 48 hours "because I use specific cooling packs that only last that long."
31. B. Detail. The text says the staff "re-routed me through Brussels," which was the first leg of his new journey.
