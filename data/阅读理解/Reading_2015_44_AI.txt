TITLE: The Laptop Nightmare
My second-hand laptop has caused me nothing but stress. I managed to buy it last summer because I asked my friends for cash instead of books for my graduation. I let a dishonest seller talk me into purchasing an older version. I realized my mistake a week later when I found a website selling a much newer model for fifty dollars less than what I had paid. However, the machine worked so well at first that I would stay on it until the battery died late at night. Fortunately, I didn't have any high-speed games installed, or I would have never gone to sleep.
Then I started having a problem with the screen that involved a strange flickering. For some reason, when I opened certain heavy applications, the screen would jump for several seconds. Gradually, this flickering began to happen during simple tasks, and to stop it, I had to close the program and restart the system. Sometimes this method would not work, and I had to lift the laptop and give it a slight shake to fix the display. I actually joked that I was getting a workout just by trying to check my emails.
When none of these tricks worked anymore, I would sit there and wait for the screen to stabilize. Eventually, I got so frustrated that I hit the keyboard with my hand, and the laptop stopped turning on completely. My visit to the IT repair shop cost me $80. It is running smoothly now, but I still worry every time I press the power button.

[ANALYSIS]
21. C. Detail/Inference. The author realized he was "fooled" because he found a "newer model for fifty dollars less."
22. A. Contextual inference. The context "I would stay on it until the battery died" implies continuous usage.
23. D. Detail. The last paragraph confirms the fix: "My visit to the IT repair shop cost me $80. It is running smoothly now."
24. D. Tone. The author's descriptions of "getting a workout" by shaking the laptop and the self-deprecating story about hitting the keyboard are written in a lighthearted, humorous style.
