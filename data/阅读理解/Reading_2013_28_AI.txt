TITLE: The Value of a Smart Purchase
Some people will go to great lengths to find a good deal, and I am definitely one of them. Take my family’s recent trip. It was my daughter’s school break, and we were flying back from Orlando. The flight was overbooked, and the airline offered us $500 per person in travel vouchers to take a flight the following day. I had a mandatory project in Seattle, so I had to leave. But that didn't mean my husband and daughter couldn't stay behind. I took the early flight with our toddler and headed home.
The next day, my husband and daughter were offered even more vouchers to wait for an even later flight. I encouraged --- well, insisted --- that they wait it out at the gate to "earn" more airline credit. Our final total: $2,000. Quite a result, right?
Now, some might judge me for this. But as a professional bargain seeker, I understand the real worth of money. These days, a truly great offer is something very few of us should ignore.
I have made a career out of finding the best value and warning others about bad marketing tricks. I have been a financial expert for a major news network for over ten years. I have written several books, including one called Smart Spender: A Survival Guide. I practice what I preach. I tell you this because there is no shame in getting the best for your money. I am very careful when spending on electronics or kids' toys. However, I won't hesitate to pay for a high-quality winter coat. It lasts much longer and is the first thing people see. I will also invest in a well-made desk. True quality is a long-term saving.
