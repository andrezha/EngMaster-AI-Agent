TITLE: A Story of Courage in the Wild West
In 1920, two young women from New York, Alice and Mary, went to a remote part of the desert to work as nurses. They had grown up in wealthy homes and wore the finest clothes. So, their decision to move to a place where people lived in tents and had very little food was a surprise. Their journey is the subject of a new book, The Desert Nurses, written by Mary’s niece.
Why did they go? They wanted to help people. However, life was very hard. They lived in a small cabin with no running water and very little heat. In the summer, the heat was unbearable, and in the winter, they were often stuck in the sand.
The book also talks about the history of the area and the strength of the women who lived there. One terrifying part of the book describes a great storm that destroyed many homes. The book ends with the women returning to the city.
The author is a wonderful writer. Her descriptions of the desert sun and the courage of the people are beautiful. She describes Alice looking out over the sand at sunset: “The sky turned deep red, and the stars began to appear one by one. The desert was quiet, except for the sound of the wind.”
