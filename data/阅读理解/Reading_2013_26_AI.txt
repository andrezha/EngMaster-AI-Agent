TITLE: The Science of Scents
Since many people’s stress levels are affected by the scents around them, it was likely only a matter of time before someone designed a fragrance shop that looked like a professional chemistry lab. Resembling a scene from a futuristic movie, Singapore’s Aroma Research Center (ARC) offers over 100 types of unique perfumes. Its founder is Julian Chen, who spent much of his childhood in his family’s small garden, always fascinated by the smell of different flowers.
If the ARC seems like a brilliant business idea, it is because Chen is not just an experienced seller but also the leader of a branding agency that works with global tech and fashion companies. This professional background clearly helps when it comes to using imagination to create various scent combinations.
The ARC’s products are "natural," produced locally and organized into 10 categories. The Relaxing Series is currently the most popular among customers. The Exotic Series --- featuring scents of wild pepper, rare woods, and green tea --- is also a favorite for those who enjoy trying something new. For "scent experts" who believe they know more about perfumes than the average person, the Collector Series uses rare ingredients from Madagascar, Brazil, and Egypt.
