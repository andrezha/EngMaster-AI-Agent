TITLE: Finding My Place in the Skate Park
When I was ten, we moved from New York to a quiet town in England. I didn't like the cold rain and felt very lonely. But then I found the local skate park. The sound of boards on the ground made me feel better. I soon made friends with other skaters. We had our own words, like “Solid,” which meant good or hello. Once, when I crashed, a friend came over and said, “Solid, man. You’ll get it next time.”
Years later, I moved back to the US. I tried skating there, but the people weren't as friendly. I stopped skating for a long time. Recently, I visited that same park in England. I saw a teenager doing tricks. I told him, “I used to skate here 20 years ago.” He nodded and said, “Solid, man.” I felt at home again.
