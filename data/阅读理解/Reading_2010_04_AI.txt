TITLE: The Warm Heart of a Small Town
There were helpful volunteers all the way. Clearly they knew at what time travelers usually arrived, and they made it their mission to stand by the rest stops, wave to strangers and offer them cold water as they drove toward the valley. Often whole groups of locals stood outside their shops and smiled as if the visitors were their old friends. This is the simple spirit of the countryside. I was deeply touched.
I had always traveled by high-speed train, so this was the first time I had taken a long road trip. I did not particularly enjoy the idea of a long drive and had brought along many podcasts to listen to. I looked around the gas station. There was not one familiar face. I sighed and sat back to check my work emails.
It was not long before I reached the mountain pass. The city was just another busy place like home, so I was tired of looking at the heavy traffic as it moved slowly. As I went beyond the city, I watched the straight rows of pine trees and miles of green fields. Then the first small town came into sight. Immediately I felt refreshed; I decided to wave back to the people on the road.
From then on, my journey became interesting. I turned off my phone and decided to enjoy the local life. Then everything seemed different. The hills seemed to welcome me. Even the old buildings were beautiful. I looked at everything as if I were seeing it for the first time.
The day passed quickly and I even forgot about the time until I reached the final stop. I looked at my watch and was surprised that it was already 4:00 pm. Soon my car pulled up at Silver Creek. I looked at the people around me. They all looked kind. When my friend arrived with a smile, I gave her a warm hug. I had never been this enthusiastic before. She seemed surprised and then her face lit up with a huge smile. We walked toward her house. I looked forward to the drive back home.

72. A. Detail Understanding. The first and last paragraphs emphasize the "smiling" and "helpful" locals who "touched" the author.
