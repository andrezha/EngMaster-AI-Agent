TITLE: Future Talent Programs
The city council and its partners offer several initiatives to help young people find internships and project opportunities. Deadlines and requirements vary by program.
Not a student? Check the official website for tools and resources designed to help individuals under 30 develop skills or launch startups throughout the year.
Community Leaders
If you live in specific districts, you might be eligible for this program. It offers two months of paid work and professional training.
Eligibility: Youth aged 15-18 in selected neighborhoods.
Startup Grant
This program provides students with business guidance and grants of up to $3,000 to launch their own summer projects.
Eligibility: Students aged 15-29, returning to school in the next term.
Nature Rangers
Apply to become a Nature Ranger and work on environmental protection projects for eight weeks during the summer.
Eligibility: Students aged 16 or 17 at the time of application, but not reaching 18 before the end of this year.
Public Service Internships
Through this initiative, students are hired for various roles within the municipal government and local community groups.
Eligibility: Students aged 15 or older. Certain roles allow for applicants up to 29 for those with physical challenges.
