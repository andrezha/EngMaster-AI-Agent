TITLE: Museums and Culture in the City
The City Gallery
The City Gallery is an award-winning art space in the heart of the city's cultural center. With three large halls, it is the home of the annual Modern Art Festival. It shows various works, including paintings, photos, and digital art from around the world.
10 Main Street
www.citygallery.com
The Jazz Hub
Every weekend, this is the city's best place for live music. Many famous jazz stars have played on this stage. If you are looking for great music and a friendly atmosphere, you should start here.
25 Harbor Way
www.jazzhub.com
Science World
Science World reopened in 2021. This building is a place where children and adults can learn about technology. It offers many hands-on activities and shows about space and nature.
50 Education Road
www.scienceworld.com
History Museum
The History Museum has been the home of local treasures for over 150 years. As the city's oldest museum, it shows the history of the region from ancient times to today. Be sure to visit during your stay.
80 Park View
www.historymuseum.com
