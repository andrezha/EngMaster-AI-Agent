TITLE: International Youth Arts Week
Next month, our university is hosting talented students from around the world to perform classic plays in their native languages. Please come and support these young performers.
Beijing Youth Group | Mandarin
This is the group’s first performance in Europe. Their work represents the energy of modern Chinese campus theater. They will perform a version of Hamlet, directed by the award-winning student director, Zhang Wei.
Date & Time: Saturday 12 Oct, 2:00pm & Sunday 13 Oct, 1:00pm & 7:00pm
Tbilisi Art School | Georgian
As one of the most respected student theaters in Georgia, founded in 1950, they often travel to international festivals. Their new production of Romeo and Juliet is led by student leader Anna Bakradze.
Date & Time: Friday 18 Oct, 2:00pm & Saturday 19 Oct, 8:00pm
Hands-on Drama | British Sign Language (BSL)
By turning the poetic words of A Midsummer Night’s Dream into the visual language of BSL, this London-based group creates a unique experience. They aim to connect deaf and hearing audiences by performing for everyone together.
Date & Time: Tuesday 22 Oct, 2:00pm & Wednesday 23 Oct, 8:00pm
Tel Aviv University | Hebrew
This group is a leader in Hebrew-language theater for students. Based in Israel, they have been performing since 1960. This production of Macbeth marks their first visit to this city.
Date & Time: Monday 28 Oct, 8:00pm & Tuesday 29 Oct, 8:00pm
